# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Planeola lets organizations (non-profits, groups, services) collect their
members' availability and build a schedule. This page explains which data
is processed on planeola.com, why, and what your rights are. Planeola is
operated from France and subject to the General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR). This page is also available in [French](/privacy?lang=fr); for
users in France, the French version prevails.

## Who is responsible for your data?

Two distinct roles:

- **Your account** (email address, password): the publisher of planeola.com,
  Thomas Gladin, is the data controller. Contact: [contact@planeola.com](mailto:contact@planeola.com).
- **An organization's data** (member records, availability, schedule): the
  organization that added you is the data controller. The publisher hosts
  and processes that data only on the organization's behalf, as a processor
  (GDPR Art. 28). For this data, contact the organization first; the
  publisher will pass on any request it receives.

## What data, and why?

**Account** — to identify you and secure access (performance of the
service, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)):

- email address, used as your login;
- password, stored only as a bcrypt hash, never in plain text;
- whether your address is verified, and temporarily a verification code
  (15 minutes) or a password-reset link (30 minutes), both also stored
  only as hashes;
- your memberships: which organizations you belong to, with which display
  name and role.

**An organization's data** — to build the schedule (on the organization's
behalf):

- member record: last name, first name, email, phone, categories,
  departure date, availability notes, remarks entered by administrators;
- declared availability, and any message attached to a submission;
- schedule assignments (date, time slot, category);
- pending invitations (invited email, email of the person inviting);
- organization settings, including the manager's email.

The "remarks" field is free text: organizations must not enter sensitive
data in it (health, opinions, etc.).

**Emails sent** — only those the service needs: verification code,
password-reset link, invitation to join an organization, and a notice to
the manager when a member changes their availability notes. No marketing
email.

**Technical logs** — for security (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)):
sign-ins, successful or not, and some errors are logged with the email
address and IP address involved. The IP address is also used, in memory
only and for one minute, to limit sign-in attempts.

## What Planeola does not do

- No advertising or analytics cookies, no third-party trackers.
- No selling or renting of data.
- Nothing is loaded from a third-party service: fonts and scripts are
  served by planeola.com itself.

Your browser keeps your session token (valid for 6 hours) and a few display
preferences in local storage. This storage is strictly necessary for the
service to work and needs no consent.

## How long?

- **Account**: as long as it exists. On request, it is deleted within
  30 days.
- **An organization's data**: as long as the organization keeps it. A
  submitted availability is deleted as soon as it is validated, ignored or
  out of date. A member who has left stays in the organization's history
  until the organization deletes them.
- **Technical logs**: 6 months at most.

## Who can access it?

- Your organization's administrators. Other members only see your name,
  your category and your schedule assignments.
- The publisher's technical providers, bound by contract:
  - hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH, server located in Finland;
  - email delivery: MailerSend (MailerLite group), data hosted in Belgium.

Data is hosted in the European Union. Should one of MailerSend's
sub-processors need access from outside the EU, that transfer is covered by
the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords and tokens stored only as hashes,
strict separation between organizations, limits on sign-in attempts.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data
portability and objection. Part of your information (profile,
availability, password) can be corrected directly in the app. For any other
request, including deleting your account, write to [contact@planeola.com](mailto:contact@planeola.com).

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint
with the CNIL, the French data protection authority
([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/)), or with the authority of the
country where you live.
